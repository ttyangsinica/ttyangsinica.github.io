Causality & Policy Evaluation Research Group

Welcome! I am Tzu-Ting Yang (楊子霆), a faculty member at the Institute of Economics, Academia Sinica (IEAS), Taipei, Taiwan.

The mission of my research group is to use state-of-the-art causal inference to provide evidence-based policy evaluation.
