Suggestive Textbooks

  • Angrist and Pischke (2009), Mostly Harmless Econometrics (MHE)

  • George Borjas (2016), Labor Economics, 7th Edition

  • Anton Korinek (2023), Generative AI for Economic Research: Use Cases and Implications for Economists
  • Anton Korinek (2025), AI Agents for Economic Research
