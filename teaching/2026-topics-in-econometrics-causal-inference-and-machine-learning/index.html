Syllabus

Suggestive Textbooks

  • Huntington-Klein, N. (2021), The Effect: An Introduction to Research Design and Causality
  • Scott Cunningham (2020), Causal Inference: The Mixtape
  • Alberto Abadie and Matias D. Cattaneo (2018), Econometric Methods for Program Evaluation
  • Angrist and Pischke (2016), Mastering Metrics: The Path from Cause to Effect (MM)
  • Angrist and Pischke (2009), Mostly Harmless Econometrics (MHE)
  • Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie and Robert Tibshirani (2017), An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in R
  • Anton Korinek (2023), Generative AI for Economic Research: Use Cases and Implications for Economists
  • Anton Korinek (2025), AI Agents for Economic Research
